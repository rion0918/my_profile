# Service icons

Downloaded 2026-10-25 from the services' official media kits. These marks identify
the halls that link to the owner's GitHub and Zenn profiles.

- GitHub: https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo
  - Archive: https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip
  - Original: `GitHub Logos/SVG/GitHub_Invertocat_White.svg`
  - Local SVG: `github-invertocat-white.svg` (unmodified)
- Zenn: https://zenn.dev/mediakit
  - Archive: https://static.zenn.studio/images/mediakit/zenn-logo.zip
  - Original: `zenn-logo/logo-only.svg`
  - Local SVG: `zenn-mark.svg` (unmodified)

The PNG textures are rasterizations of the original SVGs at 1024 pixels wide,
preserving their aspect ratios, colors and transparency. The physical sign
frames are separate from the artwork. No glow or graphic effects are applied
to the marks. GitHub and Zenn retain their respective trademark rights.
